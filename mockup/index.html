Aligned: your Invisalign, day by day

A phone-first tracker for wear time, tray swaps, progress photos and orthodontist follow-up. Everything stays on your phone. Tap through the phone on the right; it runs on example data.

Look and feel

Try it

Tap Trays out, then jump the clock past 30 minutes to see the nudge. Tap a log entry to edit it. Add photos from your camera roll on the Photos tab.

What each tab answers

  • TodayAm I on track for 22h? Live trays-out timer, a 2-hour daily out budget, streak, last 7 days.
  • TraysWhich tray am I on and when do I switch? Plan grid, swap date, swap-day checklist.
  • PhotosIs it working? Front, left and right shots per tray, plus a before/after slider.
  • DentistWhat do I bring to my next visit? Countdown, questions, symptoms log, visit notes.
  • SettingsPlan size, days per tray, wear goal and the four reminders you picked.

Open questions for v1

  • Day resetDoes a "day" end at midnight or when you go to bed? Late dinners can count against the wrong day.
  • SwapsYour orthodontist may tell you to stay on a tray longer. Should a swap shift every later date, or only that tray's?
  • BackupOn-device only means a lost phone loses the history. A JSON export to Files covers that without needing an account.
